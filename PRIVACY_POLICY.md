# Privacy Policy for TerrapinLift

**Last updated:** July 18, 2026

TerrapinLift ("the app") is developed by Basener Studio ("we," "us," "our"). This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

## The short version

TerrapinLift stores everything you log entirely on your own device. There is no account, no server, no cloud sync, and no analytics or advertising SDKs in the app. We never see your data, because it never leaves your phone unless you deliberately choose to share or export it yourself.

## What data the app stores

All of the following is saved locally on your device, in a private database the app manages:

- Logged workouts: lifts (exercise, sets, reps, weight, effort), cardio sessions (distance, duration, pace), and HIIT circuits
- Body metrics you choose to log: bodyweight, steps, sleep, soreness, and any custom metrics you create
- Menstrual cycle tracking, if you use that feature
- Progress photos you take or select, stored as image files in the app's private storage
- Profile details you enter during setup or in Settings: gender, birth year, unit preferences, and goals (used only to calculate strength-standard comparisons and readiness estimates shown back to you in the app)
- App preferences: which data set is active, layout customizations, and similar settings

None of this is transmitted to us or to any third party by the app itself. We have no server that receives it, and no way to view it.

## Permissions the app requests

- **Camera** — only used if you choose to take a progress photo. You can decline this permission and still use every other feature; photo capture just won't be available.
- **Notifications** — used only for optional daily reminders you set up yourself on Home's Checklist widget (e.g. "log sleep"). These are scheduled locally on your device; nothing about them is sent anywhere.

The app requests no location, contacts, microphone, or background data access.

## When data leaves your device

Only when you take one of these actions yourself:

- **Export** (Settings) writes a JSON file of your data and opens your device's normal share sheet, so you can save or send that file wherever you choose (email, cloud storage, another app, etc.). That sharing happens through your device's own OS, using whatever destination you pick — we don't receive a copy.
- **Sharing a link you added** — if you add a YouTube link to an exercise for form reference, tapping it opens that link in your browser or the YouTube app, same as tapping any link would.

We have no other data-sharing mechanism, no ad network, and no third-party analytics library integrated into the app.

## Data deletion

You're always in full control:

- Individual entries can be edited or deleted from within the app.
- Settings includes tools to wipe all logged data while keeping your exercise list, or to reset a data set entirely.
- Uninstalling the app deletes its private storage, including the database and any progress photos, per normal Android behavior.

## Children's privacy

TerrapinLift is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from children. Since the app doesn't transmit data anywhere regardless of who uses it, there's no data of any kind being collected from any user, child or adult.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and the revised version will be posted at the same location. Since the app doesn't collect data to begin with, we don't anticipate substantive changes to what's described here.
