# Screen: Home

The dashboard. First thing seen on open. Data-forward, per the icon → plot → words priority (see `00_UX_DESIGN.md`).

## Contents (top to bottom) — implemented

1. **Muscle status icons** (`MuscleStatusRow`) — replaces the old text-based Status cards, unifying the last piece that was still on the layer-1 recency heuristic instead of the compass. One icon per broad category (legs/back/chest/core/arms), icon *shape* carries which of 3 states applies, color carries urgency:
   - **Ready** (weight icon, green `AppColors.good`) — recovery window elapsed, or never trained; a soft "worth targeting today" nudge.
   - **Resting** (bed icon, muted grey `AppColors.textSecondary`) — still inside its typical recovery window from the last time it was trained. Deliberately *not* colored like a warning — this is the normal, expected state, nothing to act on.
   - **Overreaching** (warning-triangle icon, amber `AppColors.warn`) — RPE creeping up or e1RM dipping against its own recent average for that specific category, a stronger signal than plain rest. Computed **per category independently** (`ReadinessEngine.computeCategoryStatus`), per the user's explicit framing: legs being cooked/overreaching shouldn't block a nudge toward arms — there's no single blanket "take a rest day" verdict.
   - Tapping an icon opens a one-line plain-language explanation for that specific category. See `07_SMART_TRENDS.md` for the derivation.
2. **This Week rings** — one ring per day (last 7 days): outer arc fills by % of the daily step goal (`UserProfile.stepsGoal`, adjustable in Settings — `06_SCREEN_settings.md`, was hardcoded at 10,000), inner fill lights up if a workout was logged that day. Replaced an earlier plain checkmark strip — rings read as progress, not pass/fail.
3. **Primed for Growth** — the front+back per-muscle readiness heatmap (green scale), the app's actual "compass." Has an "i" info icon next to the title (`InfoTooltip(glossaryKey: 'readiness')`) explaining the contributing factors in plain language without spelling out the exact formula. See `07_SMART_TRENDS.md` for the full formula and `08_GLOSSARY_AND_SCIENCE.md` for the tooltip copy.
   - **Good picks right now** (`PrimedLiftsRow`, implemented) — a row of up to 5 tappable lift-name chips directly under the map, each one tapping through to that lift's detail screen. Not a ranked "best lift" list and not a "you should do X" prescription — a **greedy weighted set-cover** (`ReadinessEngine.suggestPrimedLifts`) over every exercise's muscle coverage against the currently-primed muscles (readiness ≥ 0.6), so lifts that hit mostly-overlapping primed muscles don't both get suggested (e.g. Front Squat and Back Squat both hitting primed quads — only the one covering more of what's primed earns a slot; the other only appears if it adds primed muscles the first one missed). Empty state: "Nothing particularly primed right now."
4. **Training Split** — a 100%-stacked bar chart, one bar per workout day (rest days collapsed out, not calendar-spaced), showing that day's training weighted across body parts (legs/core/chest/arms/back) by RPE-sum. A "zoom out" view sitting above the per-lift trends — see `00_UX_DESIGN.md` "Training composition chart" for the full reasoning (why RPE-sum over the timer, multi-category attribution, color choices).
5. **Strength trend lines** (customizable, implemented) — `CenteredTrendChart` (2:1 aspect) per user-chosen lift, e1RM not raw weight (see `08_GLOSSARY_AND_SCIENCE.md`), with the prediction extension on (last point at 75%, adaptive-degree polynomial trend continues). An edit-pencil next to the section header opens `HomeTrendPickerSheet`:
   - **Which lifts** — a plain checklist of every exercise (pinned ones sorted first, then alphabetical), multi-select, persisted as `home_trend_exercise_ids` (`01_DATA_MODEL.md`). Until customized (`null`/unset), falls back to pinned lifts (capped to 4), and further falls back to recently-active lifts if nothing is pinned yet (a very fresh install) — same zero-state spirit as before, just now pin-aware.
   - **How far back** — a plain number field, months, persisted as `home_trend_months` (default `6`, replacing the old hardcoded 183-day constant). No upper bound enforced beyond a sanity clamp (1-999) — if the requested range goes back further than a lift actually has history, the chart just shows everything it has; no special-casing needed since the cutoff is only ever a filter, never a hard requirement. Computed via real month subtraction (`DateTime(now.year, now.month - months, now.day)`), not a flat `× 30 days` approximation.
   - Tap-through to the lift's full detail isn't wired from these cards yet — only from the Lifts tab list and the primed-lifts chips.
6. Bottom nav bar (shared across all screens — see `00_UX_DESIGN.md` for the 5 destinations).

## Known gaps / next up
- No ordering/priority rule for the primed-lifts row beyond the greedy set-cover's own pick order (highest-coverage first) — hasn't needed one yet.
- Strength trend cards still aren't tappable through to the lift detail screen (only the Lifts tab list and primed-lifts chips are).
- The trend picker's checklist isn't searchable — fine at ~75 exercises with pinned-first sorting, may want a search field if the library keeps growing.
- No specific "random widget" ideas queued for Home yet — noted as an open slot if/when the user thinks of one.
